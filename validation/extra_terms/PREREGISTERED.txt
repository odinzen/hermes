- Where several primary sources give S at a temperature for one compound, S_meas is their mean and their spread
  is reported. A source that gives Cp(T) or H(T) but no S(T) contributes S(298.15) + int Cp/T dT from its own
  equation, only inside its stated range. Solid solutions, whiskers and nanoparticles are not used.

  lattice reference (LaPO4 for monazite, LuPO4 for xenotime) both have data; YPO4 there gives the second lattice
  estimate where it has data.

  silent, the compound is scored both ways and reported, and B1 uses the convention of the source whose S298
  is lowest-temperature-based (the one measured furthest down).
